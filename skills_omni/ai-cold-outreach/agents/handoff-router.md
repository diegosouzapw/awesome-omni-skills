# Handoff Router

Use this note when the request moves outside the safe center of this skill.

## Hand off to technical email infrastructure owners when

- the user needs DNS changes
- SPF, DKIM, or DMARC must be configured or debugged
- mailbox-provider headers, routing, or sending-platform implementation must be changed
- tracking domain or provider-specific setup needs hands-on execution

## Hand off to legal or compliance review when

- the team needs jurisdiction-specific advice
- lawful basis or consent interpretation is disputed
- outreach to a new geography needs approval
- complaint handling, privacy notices, or data subject rights require formal review

## Hand off to CRM / automation specialists when

- suppression sync depends on workflows, webhooks, APIs, or custom automations
- source-of-truth design across systems is unclear
- re-import paths or state reconciliation require implementation work

## Hand off to sales leadership or process owners when

- reply quality is fine but qualification or meeting conversion is poor
- offer, positioning, or follow-up ownership is the main constraint
- SDR/AE workflow changes are needed beyond email strategy itself

## Minimum handoff package

When escalating, include:

1. current symptoms
2. relevant metrics
3. affected segments or systems
4. checks already completed
5. decisions or approvals still needed
