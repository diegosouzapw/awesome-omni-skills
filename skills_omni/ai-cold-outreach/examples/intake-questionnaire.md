# Intake Questionnaire

Use these questions before designing or troubleshooting a cold outreach program.

## Goals

1. What business outcome matters most: replies, meetings, pipeline, reactivation, or deliverability recovery?
2. Is this a new program, an optimization project, or a recovery effort?

## Audience

3. Who is the ICP by role, company size, industry, and geography?
4. Which segments are in scope now, and which should be excluded?
5. What trigger signals indicate good timing?

## Offer and message

6. What are you offering, and what result does it create for the recipient?
7. What proof can you truthfully use?
8. What CTA is acceptable for a cold first touch?

## Data and tooling

9. Where do leads come from?
10. How are records enriched and verified?
11. Which systems store outreach state: CRM, sequencer, spreadsheets, enrichment tables?
12. Which tools are already in use?

## Compliance and suppression

13. Which geographies are being contacted?
14. Who owns legal/compliance review if needed?
15. How are opt-outs, complaints, and do-not-contact states synchronized?
16. What is the source of truth for suppression?

## Deliverability and launch readiness

17. Are sending domains and mailboxes already live, warming, or underperforming?
18. Who owns SPF, DKIM, DMARC, and mailbox infrastructure?
19. What monitoring exists for spam, complaints, bounces, and unsubscribes?

## Measurement

20. What are the current baseline metrics?
21. Which metric is most important to improve first?
22. What guardrails would force a pause or rollback?
