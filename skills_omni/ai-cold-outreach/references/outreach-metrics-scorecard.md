# Outreach Metrics Scorecard

Use this scorecard to evaluate campaign health without over-weighting vanity metrics.

## Primary outcome metrics

### Positive reply rate

Use as a core signal of message-market fit.

Questions:
- Are replies genuinely interested or merely polite?
- Which segment produces the highest-quality positive replies?

### Qualified meeting rate

Measures how often outreach converts into meetings worth taking.

Questions:
- Are meetings aligned to ICP and offer?
- Is qualification happening too late?

### Show rate

Measures whether booked meetings actually happen.

Questions:
- Is the CTA too aggressive for cold traffic?
- Is scheduling friction or handoff delay hurting attendance?

### Pipeline contribution

Use when the user has downstream attribution visibility.

Questions:
- Which segments and messages create real commercial value?
- Are reply-rich campaigns actually producing revenue opportunities?

## Guardrail metrics

### Bounce rate

Tracks list and verification quality.

### Complaint / spam rate

Tracks reputation risk.

### Unsubscribe rate

Tracks audience fit, list quality, and message tolerance.

### Time-to-first-human-follow-up

Tracks operational readiness after a positive reply.

## Metrics to treat cautiously

### Open rate

Open rate is noisy because tracking can be distorted by privacy protections, prefetching, and client behavior. Use it as a weak directional signal, not a primary KPI.

## Minimum reporting set

For most reviews, report:

- positive reply rate
- qualified meeting rate
- show rate
- bounce rate
- complaint/spam indicator
- unsubscribe rate
- top and bottom performing segments
- experiments currently running
