# KPI Formulas and Review Cadence

## Core Formulas

- **Activation rate** = activated users / eligible new users
- **Free-to-paid conversion** = paid conversions / eligible free users or trial accounts
- **PQL rate** = PQL accounts / eligible active accounts
- **PQL-to-opportunity** = opportunities from PQLs / PQLs
- **PQL-to-close** = closed-won from PQLs / PQLs
- **CAC** = sales and marketing spend / new customers acquired in period
- **CAC payback** = CAC / monthly gross profit per customer
- **Expansion rate** = expansion revenue from cohort / starting cohort revenue
- **Logo churn** = lost customers / starting customers in period

Use the team's internal finance definitions where they differ, but keep the definition explicit.

## Suggested Review Cadence

| Team stage | Weekly review | Monthly review |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Founder-led / pre-scale | activation, TTFV, customer conversations, top experiment results | conversion by segment, pricing friction, assumptions log refresh |
| Seed / early hybrid | activation, PQL volume and quality, sales timing, pipeline bottlenecks | CAC/payback trend, segment economics, expansion signal quality |
| Growth stage | segment scorecards, sales-assisted conversion, pipeline efficiency | motion mix, retention/expansion, hiring and channel allocation |

## Traffic-Light Threshold Method

When external benchmarks are weak or non-comparable, use:

- **Green:** better than current baseline by agreed target
- **Yellow:** roughly at baseline or inconclusive
- **Red:** materially worse than baseline or declining across two review periods

Document thresholds by segment rather than forcing one company-wide number.
