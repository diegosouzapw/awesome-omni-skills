# Trial vs Freemium Decision Guide

## Choose Freemium When

- users can get real value with low marginal support cost
- the product has natural usage or collaboration triggers that create upgrades
- free access improves distribution or network effects
- the market expects some self-serve entry point

## Choose Free Trial When

- value becomes obvious quickly once the product is used properly
- support or infrastructure cost for free users is meaningful
- urgency improves decision-making
- enterprise buyers want proof before procurement but not an unlimited free plan

## Consider Reverse Trial When

- premium features demonstrate value clearly during guided early use
- you want low-friction onboarding but still need a durable free experience later

## Warning Signs

### Freemium warning signs

- many signups but weak activation
- free plan solves the whole job for most users
- no clear feature or usage threshold creates upgrade pressure

### Trial warning signs

- trial duration starts before setup is complete
- users spend most of the trial configuring instead of realizing value
- card requirement suppresses too much qualified demand
