# AE Social Selling Plan

## Objective

Support pipeline creation through disciplined account research, relevant engagement, personalized outreach, and CRM attribution.

## Weekly operating model

### Monday: list and trigger review
- review saved searches
- check account and lead alerts
- prioritize target accounts by fit and trigger strength

### Tuesday: engage and observe
- engage selectively with relevant buyer posts
- capture language, priorities, and trigger events
- log notable observations for outreach

### Wednesday: outreach block
- send personalized connection requests where context exists
- follow up with value-first DMs to accepted connections
- stop at one clear ask per thread

### Thursday: meeting conversion
- review active conversations
- identify where buyer intent is visible
- propose short calls only when relevance is confirmed

### Friday: CRM and metrics review
- tag new meetings by source
- update opportunity influence fields
- review acceptance, reply, and meeting rates by segment

## AE guardrails

- prioritize fit and relevance over volume
- do not treat social as a separate untracked side channel
- keep the CRM handoff clean when opportunities form
- use content engagement as signal, not proof of buying intent
