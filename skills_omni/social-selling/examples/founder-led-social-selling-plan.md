# Founder-Led Social Selling Plan

## Objective

Create a founder-led LinkedIn motion that builds credibility, starts relevant conversations, and supports pipeline generation.

## Weekly cadence

### Monday
- review target buyers and recent market triggers
- draft 2 to 3 posts tied to buyer pain, proof, or founder POV
- identify 10 to 15 priority accounts to monitor

### Tuesday
- publish one post
- reply thoughtfully to comments
- engage with a small number of ICP posts with real insight

### Wednesday
- review who engaged with content
- send a limited number of personalized follow-ups where context exists
- update CRM notes for any real conversations

### Thursday
- publish second post or a proof asset
- review Sales Navigator alerts or account changes
- note any patterns in buyer language

### Friday
- review the week by funnel stage
- identify one content theme to repeat and one to retire
- log meetings, influenced opportunities, and attribution gaps

## Founder priorities

- optimize profile before increasing posting frequency
- lean into authentic POV and operator insight
- avoid sounding like a scaled outbound engine
- use DMs as conversation starters, not aggressive closing motions
