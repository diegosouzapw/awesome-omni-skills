# Employee Advocacy Rollout

## Goal

Create a small, repeatable employee-advocacy program that supports social selling without forcing inauthentic posting behavior.

## Phase 1: pilot

- choose 5 to 10 employees already active or willing
- optimize their profiles first
- define approved themes and tone guardrails
- explain how advocacy supports credibility and pipeline

## Phase 2: enablement

- provide weekly topic suggestions, not mandatory scripts
- encourage personal commentary and lived experience
- share proof assets employees can reference safely
- track participation and meaningful engagement

## Phase 3: scale

- identify which themes and roles create real business impact
- highlight good examples internally
- improve training for commenting, DM follow-up, and attribution

## Guardrails

- do not require copy-paste posting
- do not simulate grassroots enthusiasm
- do not judge success by activity volume alone
- tie the program to conversations, meetings, and influenced pipeline
