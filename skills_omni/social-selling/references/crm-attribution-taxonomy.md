# CRM Attribution Taxonomy

Use this reference to standardize social-selling attribution.

## Required fields

- Lead source
- First-touch source
- Influence source
- Conversion source
- Meeting owner
- Opportunity owner
- Campaign or motion type
- Notes on trigger or context

## Recommended source labels

### Lead source
- LinkedIn - inbound
- LinkedIn - outbound
- LinkedIn - content engagement
- LinkedIn - Sales Navigator
- Employee advocacy
- Company page
- Other social

### Influence source
- Social comment thread
- Social DM
- Content reply
- Shared resource
- Trigger-event outreach

### Conversion source
- Meeting booked from DM
- Meeting booked from content engagement
- Meeting booked from referral after social interaction
- Opportunity created after social touch sequence

## Ownership rules

- The meeting owner is the person who ran the conversation.
- The opportunity owner is the account executive or responsible seller.
- If social created awareness but another channel booked the meeting, preserve both first-touch and conversion-touch fields.

## Audit checklist

- Can recent meetings be grouped by social source consistently?
- Are multiple people using the same labels?
- Is influenced pipeline distinguishable from sourced pipeline?
- Are handoffs from social to sales clear?
- Can leadership inspect conversion rates by source label?
