# Sales Navigator Setup Checklist

Use this checklist to turn Sales Navigator into a repeatable prospecting workflow.

## 1. Define ICP fields

Document the fields that matter most:

- title / function
- seniority
- company size
- industry
- geography
- business model
- technology environment if relevant
- trigger events
- exclusions

## 2. Build account lists

Create account segments such as:

- Tier 1: high-fit named accounts
- Tier 2: good-fit expansion accounts
- Tier 3: nurture or monitor accounts

For each account list, define:

- why it exists
- who owns it
- review cadence
- entry criteria
- removal criteria

## 3. Build lead lists

For each list, define:

- account segment linked to it
- buyer roles included
- trigger conditions
- outreach priority
- handoff owner

## 4. Save key searches

Save searches for:

- core ICP
- recent job changes
- active posters or recently visible leads
- strategic accounts by role cluster
- event- or trigger-based searches

## 5. Establish alert review cadence

Review at least weekly:

- job changes
- posting activity
- company news
- list growth
- new search matches

Capture any outreach trigger in CRM or task management.

## 6. Weekly hygiene review

| Check | Questions |
| --- | --- |
| lead quality | Are too many leads being disqualified? |
| account fit | Are accounts aligned to current strategy? |
| list sprawl | Which lists are no longer useful? |
| trigger usefulness | Which alerts create real conversation opportunities? |
| workflow follow-through | Are insights turning into outreach or content actions? |

## 7. Rules of use

- Keep CRM as the source of truth for opportunities and pipeline stages.
- Use Sales Navigator for discovery, monitoring, and prioritization.
- Avoid overbuilding filters before validating lead quality.
- Prefer smaller, high-signal lists over large, vague collections.
- Review and prune lists on a schedule.
