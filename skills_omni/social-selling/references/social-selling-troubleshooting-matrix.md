# Social Selling Troubleshooting Matrix

| Funnel stage | Symptoms | Likely causes | Checks | Corrective actions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Profile | profile views but few conversations | unclear positioning, weak proof, poor CTA | review headline, About, Featured, recommendations | rewrite buyer-facing messaging and add stronger proof assets |
| Prospecting | large lead lists but poor fit | broad ICP, weak exclusions, no hygiene | inspect disqualification reasons and list quality | narrow filters and split by priority |
| Content | impressions without qualified engagement | broad topics, low relevance, weak POV | inspect who engages and whether CTA fits | narrow themes and improve proof or CTA |
| Engagement | comments do not turn into DMs | low specificity, no follow-up ownership | review comment quality and follow-up timing | comment with substance and define who follows up |
| DMs | low acceptance or reply rate | generic requests, pitch-first messaging, too many touches | sample recent requests and first messages | personalize, reduce friction, add stop rules |
| Meetings | replies happen but meetings do not | ask comes too early, no problem clarity | inspect reply threads for intent signals | confirm need first, then offer a short call |
| Pipeline | meetings occur but revenue impact is unclear | poor CRM labeling, weak handoff discipline | audit recent opportunities | standardize source fields and ownership |
| Team adoption | inconsistent employee participation | unclear expectations, low confidence, weak enablement | compare activity and outcomes by participant | start with pilot group and simple enablement |

## Use notes

- Diagnose one funnel stage at a time.
- Fix the earliest broken stage before optimizing later stages.
- Prefer observable evidence over assumptions.
- Document changes and review impact after one operating cycle.
