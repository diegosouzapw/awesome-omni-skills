## 5. About section rewrite inputs

### Hook
- What problem does the buyer recognize immediately?
- Why does it matter now?

### Method
- What is your approach or framework?
- What makes it distinct?

### Proof
- What customer examples, metrics, or credibility signals are safe to mention?

### CTA
- What low-friction next step should a qualified buyer take?
