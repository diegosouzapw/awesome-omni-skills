# Platform Policy and Automation Guardrails

Use this note before recommending tools or scaling outreach.

## Core guardrails

- Follow official platform policies and terms.
- Do not recommend scraping or exporting data through prohibited means.
- Do not recommend fake accounts, impersonation, or identity masking.
- Do not recommend engagement pods or coordinated fake activity.
- Do not recommend browser automation or workflow designs intended to mimic users at scale without clear platform approval.

## Safer tool categories

| Category | Relative risk | Typical use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Native platform workflows | lowest | profile updates, manual outreach, saved searches, alerts |
| Read-only analytics | lower | reporting, post analysis |
| Writing assistance | lower | drafting posts or messages that are still manually reviewed |
| Approved CRM sync / compliant integrations | medium | contact sync, activity logging |
| Bulk or behavior-mimicking outreach automation | high | mass connection requests, automated follow-up, evasion-oriented workflows |

## Recommendation rule

If a tool is mentioned:

1. state that the operator must verify current compliance with platform terms
2. verify data handling, security, and contractual suitability
3. avoid describing risky tooling as inherently safe
4. prefer manual or native workflows when there is uncertainty

## Outreach safety rules

- personalize messages
- be transparent about commercial intent when relevant
- avoid deceptive urgency or false familiarity
- stop after repeated non-response
- move to nurture rather than pressure

## Escalation triggers

Stop and reassess if the request asks for:

- scraping or mass export
- rotating accounts or identities
- bypassing platform limits
- stealth automation
- manufactured engagement signals
