# Renewal Risk Review Example

## Scenario

An enterprise account renews in 75 days. Usage is stable, but the executive sponsor left, support sentiment declined, and there is no recent proof-of-value summary.

## Example assessment

```text
Renewal risk: medium-high

Why:
- usage alone does not offset stakeholder risk
- executive sponsor coverage is weak
- support friction is unresolved
- proof of value is not packaged for the buyer

Immediate actions:
1. rebuild stakeholder map within 7 days
2. prepare proof-of-value summary tied to agreed outcomes
3. review open support and product issues with owners and dates
4. decide whether executive sponsorship is needed before day 60

Watch metrics:
- sponsor replacement identified
- unresolved ticket count trending down
- renewal conversation formally opened
- value summary delivered and acknowledged
```
