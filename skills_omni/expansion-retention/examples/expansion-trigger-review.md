# Expansion Trigger Review Example

## Scenario

A collaboration SaaS company wants to trigger upsell outreach when an account:

- reaches 80% of seat capacity, and
- adds 3 or more teammates in 14 days

## Review

### Why this may be promising

- seat pressure suggests real usage, not just curiosity
- teammate growth may indicate spreading adoption
- the two signals together are stronger than either alone

### False-positive risks

- temporary project spike rather than durable expansion need
- team invites from a trial or implementation burst
- usage by non-buying users without manager awareness

### Recommended owner

- **Product** for immediate in-app guidance
- **CS or sales** only after the account also shows sustained activation or a known buyer contact

### Recommended experiment

Run a 30-day review with:

- triggered accounts
- conversion to meeting or upgrade
- ignored prompts
- accounts that triggered but later contracted
- accounts that upgraded without triggering

### Suggested output

```text
Trigger quality: moderate, worth testing
Primary owner: Product first, then CS for qualified accounts
Main risk: signal may reflect temporary collaboration spikes
Success metric: upgrade or qualified expansion conversation rate
Review cadence: monthly until false-positive rate stabilizes
```
