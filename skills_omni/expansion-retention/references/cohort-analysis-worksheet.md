# Cohort Analysis Worksheet

Use this worksheet when blended metrics hide where churn or expansion is happening.

## Required cuts

Review retention and expansion by:

- start month or quarter
- segment
- pricing plan
- ACV band
- acquisition channel
- implementation path
- lifecycle stage

## Questions to answer

1. Which cohorts retain worst by month 1, month 3, month 6, and renewal?
2. Which segment shows the highest contraction even when logo retention is stable?
3. Which plan expands most often, and after what usage pattern?
4. Does implementation-assisted onboarding outperform self-serve onboarding?
5. Are there channels that bring lower-fit customers who churn early?

## Interpretation guide

- Early drop-off usually points to onboarding, activation, or poor-fit acquisition.
- Flat adoption with low churn but no expansion may indicate weak packaging or trigger design.
- Strong enterprise retention with weak SMB retention usually means touch-model mismatch.
- Good overall NRR can still coexist with bad new-logo cohorts.
