# Outreach Compliance Guardrails

Use this checklist before launching automated retention, win-back, or expansion outreach.

## Minimum checks

- confirm the contact source is appropriate for the intended outreach
- confirm unsubscribe or opt-out handling works
- maintain suppression lists
- avoid messaging based on sensitive or opaque profiling
- minimize customer data fields to what is required
- ensure content reflects the actual relationship and context
- verify the audience is not receiving contradictory campaigns

## Profiling and automation caution

Behavior-based retention systems often rely on profiling and automated decision support. Review whether the workflow requires transparency, additional notice, or a human review step in your jurisdiction and operating model.

## Deliverability caution

Poor audience hygiene or over-frequent re-engagement can damage sender reputation. Start narrow, test carefully, and monitor complaints, opens, clicks, replies, unsubscribes, and bounce rates.

## Red flags

Pause launch if any of these are true:

- no clear unsubscribe path
- unclear lawful basis or contact provenance
- suppressed contacts are not reliably excluded
- segmentation depends on low-confidence or sensitive signals
- the campaign targets former prospects with no clear relevance update
