# Renewal Readiness Checklist

Use this checklist to avoid late-stage renewal surprises.

## 180 days before renewal

- confirm owner
- review health trend
- identify open product, legal, security, or support risks
- confirm stakeholder map is current

## 120 days before renewal

- prepare proof-of-value summary
- review adoption and usage trend
- identify expansion possibilities separately from base renewal risk
- confirm economic buyer and champion coverage

## 90 days before renewal

- open formal renewal conversation
- document commercial path and expected obstacles
- confirm unresolved issues with owners and dates

## 60 days before renewal

- review contract terms and procurement path
- escalate unresolved blockers
- decide whether executive sponsorship is required

## 30 days before renewal

- confirm signature path
- check for silent stakeholders or sudden risk signals
- prepare save plan if confidence is low

## Risk review prompts

- Can we clearly show value achieved?
- Do we know who signs, who influences, and who might block?
- Are there unresolved product gaps or support failures?
- Is this a pure renewal, a down-sell risk, or an expansion opportunity?
- What is the escalation threshold and who owns it?
