# Decomposition Router

Use this note when the task needs deliberate handoff.

## Route to Prerequisite Skills

- Route to `@domain-analysis` when bounded contexts, context maps, or capability ownership are disputed.
- Route to `@component-identification-sizing` when there is no trustworthy inventory of modules/components.
- Route to dependency analysis work when extraction order is blocked by missing coupling evidence.

## Route to Adjacent Skills

- Route to API/service design once a candidate slice is selected and interfaces must be defined.
- Route to observability or platform-readiness skills when deployment, dashboards, alerts, or auth controls are the blocker.
- Route to release/rollback planning when cutover sequencing and coexistence mechanics become the primary task.
- Route to security/compliance review when regulated data or policy constraints change extraction order.

## Handoff Rule

When handing off, preserve:

- business goals
- current-state findings
- candidate-slice decisions
- explicit assumptions
- unresolved risks
- deferred items and why they were deferred
