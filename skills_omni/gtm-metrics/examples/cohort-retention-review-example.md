# Cohort Retention Review Example

## Scenario

A hybrid AI product reports topline NRR of 101%. Leadership believes expansion is strong, but finance is concerned that retention is weakening.

## Bad analysis

- quote the topline NRR only
- compare it to an external median
- conclude retention is acceptable

## Better analysis

Segment by:
- signup cohort
- plan tier
- acquisition motion
- customer segment

## Example diagnosis

- older enterprise cohorts: stable GRR, moderate expansion
- newer self-serve cohorts: fast initial activation but weaker 90-day retention
- usage-based segment: strong expansion among activated accounts, but many low-usage accounts never reach value

## Likely conclusion

Topline NRR hides two different realities:
- expansion in a mature segment is offsetting churn in newer cohorts
- onboarding and TTFV are the real issues, not only account management

## Recommended output

1. Keep topline NRR for executive summary.
2. Add cohort NRR / GRR by segment.
3. Add TTFV and activation depth for new cohorts.
4. Flag which cohorts need product onboarding fixes versus CS expansion plays.
