## Core calculations

- **AI cost of revenue:** total included AI delivery cost / relevant revenue
- **Gross margin after AI:** (revenue - COGS - included AI costs) / revenue
- **Cost per AI action:** total included AI costs / total AI actions
- **Revenue per AI action or unit:** relevant revenue / total AI actions or units
- **Contribution per unit:** revenue per unit - cost per unit

## Reliability checks

- Are support and review operations excluded unintentionally?
- Are platform-wide costs mixed with product-line costs?
- Is the numerator and denominator aligned to the same cohort or period?
- Is ROAI using attribution assumptions that need to be stated?
