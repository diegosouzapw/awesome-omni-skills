# Video Outreach Privacy and Authenticity Checklist

Use this checklist before launching AI-assisted or AI-generated outreach.

## Scope

This is operational guidance, not legal advice.

## Prospect Data Minimization

- Collect only the prospect data needed to personalize the asset.
- Avoid storing unnecessary screenshots, scraped artifacts, or personal details.
- Remove research notes that are no longer needed.
- Keep retention aligned with your organization's policy.

## Direct Marketing Hygiene

- Confirm the channel and region are appropriate for outbound use.
- Make suppression, opt-out, or unsubscribe handling operationally clear.
- Do not continue sending after a clear opt-out or objection.
- Make sure CRM and sequence tools reflect suppression status correctly.

## Authenticity Review

- Decide whether the asset is human-recorded, AI-assisted, or AI-generated.
- Do not imply a materially AI-generated video was manually recorded if that would mislead the recipient.
- Review whether synthetic voice, avatar, or likeness use complies with internal brand and policy expectations.
- Obtain appropriate consent and approval for any cloned voice, face, or likeness used.

## Risk Questions

Answer these before launch:

1. Would a reasonable recipient feel misled about how this video was made?
2. Is the personalization actually relevant, or only cosmetically personalized?
3. Are any screenshots or on-screen assets exposing unnecessary personal or sensitive data?
4. Is the synthetic-media choice appropriate for this deal size and buyer seniority?
5. Is there a safer human-recorded fallback for high-trust segments?

## Escalate for Review When

- the outreach targets regulated industries or public-sector buyers
- synthetic likeness or cloned voice will be used broadly
- legal, compliance, or procurement stakeholders are primary recipients
- the workflow depends on scraped or enriched personal data of uncertain provenance
