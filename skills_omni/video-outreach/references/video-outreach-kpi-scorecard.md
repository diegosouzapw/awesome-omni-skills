# Video Outreach KPI Scorecard

Track video outreach as an attributed funnel, not a vanity dashboard.

## Core Funnel

`videos sent -> delivered -> opens -> plays -> 50% watch -> replies -> meetings -> opportunities -> closed-won`

## Core Metrics

| Metric | Formula | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Delivery rate | delivered / sent | Separates list or sender issues from creative issues |
| Open rate | opens / delivered | Indicates subject line, sender reputation, and timing |
| Play rate | plays / opens | Indicates thumbnail strength and click motivation |
| 50% watch rate | 50% watches / plays | Indicates whether the opening and pacing work |
| Reply rate | replies / delivered | Primary early conversion signal |
| Meeting rate | meetings / delivered | More meaningful than plays alone |
| Opportunity rate | opportunities / delivered | Ties outreach to pipeline creation |
| Video-to-meeting ratio | sent / meetings | Simple efficiency measure |
| Pipeline per 100 sends | pipeline value / sent * 100 | Helps compare motions economically |

## Weekly Review Questions

1. Which segments had the best play rate?
2. Which motions produced the strongest 50% watch rate?
3. Which CTA produced the highest meeting conversion?
4. Did human-recorded and AI-generated variants perform differently by persona?
5. Where did viewers drop off?
6. Were replays or internal shares correlated with pipeline creation?
7. Did video improve outcomes versus the text-only control?

## Recommended Review Cadence

- Daily: send health, delivery issues, obvious failures
- Weekly: watch patterns, replies, meetings, segment comparison
- Monthly: opportunity creation, influenced pipeline, motion ROI

## Baseline Guidance

Use your own baseline first. Do not assume vendor benchmark claims will hold in your market.

A strong review compares:

- current video motion vs prior text-only motion
- human-recorded vs AI-assisted
- one-to-one personalized vs segmented personalization
- cold outreach vs post-demo recap vs proposal walkthrough

## Notes on Tracking Quality

Open and watch analytics may be incomplete because of privacy protections, client blocking, or platform differences. Treat them as directional and rely on meetings, opportunities, and stage movement for decision-making.
