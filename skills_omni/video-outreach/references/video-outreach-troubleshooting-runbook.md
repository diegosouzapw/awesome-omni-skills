# Video Outreach Troubleshooting Runbook

## Problem: Low open rate

**Symptoms:** Good target list, but few recipients open the message.

**Check:**
- SPF, DKIM, DMARC
- domain and mailbox reputation
- send volume and pacing
- subject line relevance
- excessive images or tracking links

**Response:**
- simplify the email body
- reduce image/link density
- test a text-first variant
- rotate subject-line patterns carefully

## Problem: Open rate is acceptable, but play rate is low

**Symptoms:** People open, but they do not click into the asset.

**Check:**
- thumbnail clarity
- visible value proposition
- landing-page speed
- number of asks in the email
- whether the thumbnail or GIF renders consistently

**Response:**
- test a screenshot-style thumbnail
- shorten the surrounding copy
- use one CTA only
- add a plain-text watch link

## Problem: Drop-off in first 10 seconds

**Symptoms:** Plays happen, but average watch percentage is weak.

**Check:**
- first line specificity
- opening filler
- whether the deliverable is visible early
- pacing and audio quality

**Response:**
- lead with their name, company, or trigger
- remove generic intro language
- reveal the artifact earlier
- shorten the video

## Problem: Good watch rate, weak replies

**Symptoms:** Viewers consume the asset, but meetings do not follow.

**Check:**
- CTA clarity
- next-step friction
- whether the content is interesting but not actionable
- follow-up timing

**Response:**
- offer one narrow next step
- send same-day text follow-up for high-watch cases
- reduce calendar friction if appropriate

## Problem: AI-generated videos underperform

**Symptoms:** Scaled synthetic motion gets fewer replies or lower trust.

**Check:**
- segment trust sensitivity
- unnatural pronunciation or lip sync
- over-personalized or uncanny presentation
- mismatch between creative style and buyer expectations

**Response:**
- move strategic accounts back to human-recorded assets
- tighten QA on names and company references
- simplify the motion to AI-assisted rather than fully synthetic

## Problem: CRM attribution is incomplete

**Symptoms:** The team cannot tell whether video affects meetings or pipeline.

**Check:**
- email logging configuration
- sequence sync
- contact association rules
- opportunity attribution rules

**Response:**
- define one reporting path before scaling
- standardize campaign naming
- review a small sample manually to verify event flow
