# Discovery Intake Questionnaire

Use this at the start of any video-outreach engagement.

## Business Context

1. What outcome matters most: meetings, faster deal progression, or fewer intro calls?
2. What is the average deal size?
3. Which personas are being targeted?
4. Which regions and languages are in scope?

## Volume and Process

5. How many prospects will receive video touches each week or month?
6. Is the team already using video today?
7. Is outreach one-to-one, segmented, or high-volume automated?

## Stack

8. Which CRM is in use?
9. Which sequencer is in use?
10. Which video tools are already approved?
11. Are interactive demo tools available?

## Policy and Trust

12. Is synthetic voice, avatar, or cloned likeness permitted?
13. Are there compliance-sensitive regions or industries involved?
14. Is there a documented suppression or unsubscribe process?

## Asset Readiness

15. What useful deliverable can be shown to the prospect?
16. What proof point or customer example is safe to mention?
17. What is the single preferred CTA?
