# Sequence Design Worksheet

Map the outreach motion before execution.

| Day | Channel | Asset | Goal | Trigger for next action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Email | Personalized video | Earn first engagement | Open / play / reply |
| 3 | Email | Text follow-up | Recover missed first touch | No reply |
| 5 | LinkedIn / voice note | Short follow-up | Add channel variety | No reply |
| 8 | Email | Interactive demo or use-case walkthrough | Show product context | Play / demo completion |
| 12 | Email | Value-add insight | Rebuild relevance | No reply |
| 15 | Email / LinkedIn | Breakup note or short video | Close loop respectfully | Final response |

## Design Questions

1. Which touches should use video?
2. Which touches should stay text-only?
3. What is the primary CTA for each stage?
4. What follow-up rule applies for 50%+ watch?
5. What follow-up rule applies for replay or internal share?
6. When should the workflow escalate to a live meeting?
