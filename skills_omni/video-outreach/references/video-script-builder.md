# Video Script Builder

Use this template to draft a concise outreach video.

## Cold Outreach Script

### Hook (0:00-0:05)
- Name the prospect or company.
- Reference one specific trigger.

Example:
`[Name], noticed [Company] just [trigger].`

### Relevance (0:05-0:15)
- Explain why you made this for them.

Example:
`I put together a quick [audit / walkthrough / teardown] because I think you're likely dealing with [specific issue].`

### Visible Deliverable (0:15-0:45)
- Show the artifact early.
- Walk through 1-2 useful findings.

### Proof (0:45-0:55)
- Add one relevant proof point, example, or observed outcome.

### CTA (0:55-1:00)
- Ask for one next step only.

Example:
`If this is relevant, happy to walk through the rest live.`

## Recap / Proposal Variant

- recap the agreed problem
- restate the 2-3 most relevant points
- identify the decision or next step
- make it easy to forward internally

## Final Check

- Is the first sentence specific?
- Is the deliverable visible early?
- Is there only one CTA?
- Can this be shortened by 10-20%?
