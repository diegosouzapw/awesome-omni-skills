# Video Outreach Decision Matrix

Use this matrix to choose the right outreach motion before selecting a tool.

| Scenario | Volume | ACV / deal sensitivity | Recommended asset | Personalization depth | Preferred recording style | Tool options | Trust notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Founder-led outbound | Low | High | Deliverable walkthrough | Very high | Human-recorded | Loom, Sendspark | Best when authenticity matters most |
| SDR moderate-volume outbound | Medium | Medium | Personalized intro + short proof | Medium | Human-recorded or AI-assisted | Sendspark, Loom | Avoid synthetic-heavy motion for trust-sensitive lists |
| High-volume outbound | High | Low to medium | Short intro + linked asset | Segment-level | AI-assisted or AI-generated | Tavus, HeyGen, Potion | Only use with strong review and policy alignment |
| Multilingual campaign | High | Medium | Localized intro + localized landing asset | Medium | AI-generated with review | HeyGen, Tavus | Review translation quality and brand fit |
| Pre-demo async replacement | Low to medium | Medium to high | Intro video + interactive demo | Medium | Human or narrated demo | Loom, Supademo, Navattic | Good when product experience matters |
| Post-demo recap | Low | High | Screen-share recap | High | Human-recorded | Loom | Best for internal forwarding |
| Proposal walkthrough | Low | High | Proposal review video | High | Human-recorded | Loom | Keep CTA specific and next-step clear |
| Champion enablement | Low | High | Summary video + demo + one-pager | High | Human-recorded | Loom, Navattic, Supademo | Optimize for forwarding, not just first viewer |
| Breakup touch | Medium | Low to medium | Short webcam-only close-the-loop | Light | Human-recorded | Loom, Sendspark | Keep effort low and respectful |

## Fast Decision Rules

### Prefer human-recorded when

- the account is strategic
- the buyer is senior or trust-sensitive
- the message references a meaningful custom deliverable
- the team wants to avoid authenticity concerns

### Prefer AI-assisted when

- the team needs moderate scale
- the core body can be reused
- light personalization is enough
- the brand is comfortable with limited synthetic assistance

### Prefer AI-generated when

- scale or language coverage is the main constraint
- the workflow has strong approvals and QA
- the recipient is unlikely to feel misled by the format
- fallback options exist for high-trust segments

## Primary Metrics by Motion

- Founder-led outbound: replies, meetings booked, opportunity creation
- SDR moderate-volume: plays, replies, meetings
- High-volume AI motion: delivered-to-play rate, meetings by segment, unsubscribe/suppression rate
- Async demo: completion rate, follow-up meeting conversion
- Post-demo recap: internal shares, progression to next stage
- Proposal walkthrough: stakeholder engagement, next meeting conversion, stage advancement
