# Example: Post-Demo Recap and Champion Enablement

## Scenario

- demo already completed
- buyer needs to share internally
- next step is stakeholder alignment

## Recommended Packet

1. 2-4 minute recap video
2. interactive demo tailored to the use case
3. one-page summary of business value
4. short FAQ or objection-handling note

## Handoff to Live Call

Move back to live when:

- multiple stakeholders engage
- pricing or security questions emerge
- the buyer asks for a tailored deep dive

## KPI Focus

- internal shares
- follow-up meeting conversion
- stage progression
