# Example: Multilingual AI Video Campaign

## Scenario

- outreach across English, Spanish, and French
- repeated value proposition
- moderate to high volume
- concern about authenticity risk

## Recommended Motion

- use AI-generated or translated video only where volume and language coverage justify it
- review pronunciation, translation quality, and brand tone before launch
- keep a human-recorded fallback for strategic or trust-sensitive accounts

## Required Checks

- synthetic-media approval is documented
- suppression handling is operationally clear
- translations are reviewed by a human
- names and company references are QA checked

## Fallback Pattern

For high-trust accounts, switch to:

- human-recorded English video
- localized text follow-up
- interactive demo with localized captions or summary
