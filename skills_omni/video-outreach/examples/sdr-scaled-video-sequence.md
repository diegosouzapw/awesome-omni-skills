# Example: SDR Scaled Video Sequence

## Scenario

- 200 prospects per month
- mid-market targets
- one SDR team
- goal is meeting creation

## Recommended Motion

- use human-recorded template with moderate personalization, or AI-assisted first line if approved
- keep first-touch video to 30-60 seconds
- use one CTA only

## Follow-Up Logic

- no open: review subject line and sender health, then try another channel
- open, no play: improve thumbnail and surrounding copy
- 50%+ watch: same-day text follow-up
- replay or share: prioritize for live outreach

## KPI Focus

- delivered-to-open rate
- open-to-play rate
- reply rate
- meeting rate
