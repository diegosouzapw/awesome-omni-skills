# Example: Founder-Led Made-This-For-You Packet

## Scenario

- 20 enterprise prospects per month
- founder is willing to record individually
- high trust sensitivity
- goal is to create qualified first meetings

## Recommended Motion

- human-recorded webcam + screen share
- 45-90 second custom walkthrough
- one useful deliverable per account
- one CTA: reply if worth a deeper walkthrough

## Sequence

- Day 1: personalized video email
- Day 3: text-only follow-up referencing the deliverable
- Day 5: LinkedIn follow-up
- Day 8: short insight email if no reply
- Day 15: close-the-loop note

## Why this works

This motion favors credibility over scale. The custom deliverable does most of the selling work.
